## Context and decisions

Current attendee data lives in `event_guests`; guest UUIDs and nullable `importId` values are already returned in the event-detail DTO. Imports are append-only and event-scoped. The roster is rendered by the Event Detail attendee table; current event guest rows do not have an optimistic-lock revision. F01's access contract requires F03 edits to use expected revisions while keeping identity links separate from guest replacement revisions.

Confirmed implementation decisions:

- Canonical person identity is event-local. Do not create a cross-event People directory.
- Keep imported guest records and identifiers. Store canonical identity separately and link guest rows to the canonical person; do not rewrite/delete import history.
- Suggest candidates from exact normalized email or phone only; never infer identity from names alone.
- Flag phone syntax only, preserve any explicit country code, and do not claim verification or reachability.
- Require an explicit disposition for every conflicting existing match before applying an identity link.
- Owners and Organizers may mutate attendee data. Operators retain F01's read-only roster access; Wefters do not receive the roster.
- Do not scaffold profile, matching, or meeting-result tables that belong to later features. Preserve guest references and enforce F03's conflict rule wherever related records are present.

No F03 active plan existed when this plan was prepared.
